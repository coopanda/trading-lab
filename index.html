Sources & licensing
Standalone app in coopanda/trading-lab. Retains the 11 MIT-licensed tools from Jakeschincariol/trader-skill.
Prices: unofficial Yahoo Finance daily OHLC, Stooq fallback. Filings/fundamentals: SEC EDGAR submissions, 13F information tables and company facts. Congress: official House Clerk annual PTR index and original PDFs. Recent headlines: Yahoo Finance RSS.
Historical news is not guaranteed. An optional ALPHAVANTAGE_API_KEY enables provider-supported archives; provider limits apply. Timestamped headline JSON can be imported into a replay. Missing feeds and blocked requests are visibly reported.
Privacy & safety
No login, brokerage, real money, personalized recommendation, or tax filing. Paper journal, lots, target weights and alerts live in localStorage in this browser only. Other people on this browser can see them.
Public deployment: do not enter account numbers, credentials, or private financial documents. Clearing browser storage deletes local records. Network research goes to the server/public data providers; no private portfolio is uploaded by the workbench.
Indicators and conservative paper execution have built-in deterministic tests.
